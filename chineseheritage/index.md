![Ho Chee bust](https://irumble.com/chineseheritage/hochee.jpg)

![Ho Chee in Chinese](https://irumble.com/chineseheritage/hochee-chinese.png)

Ho Chee was born in 1789 at Hyan-Shan in Canton, China (now known as Guangzhou in the province of Guangdong). He was the son of Ho Foo, a mandarin. It is likely that Ho Chee worked with or for the East India Company (EIC) in Canton and Macao and had an official Government sponsored post.

[via HoChee Cottages, Dormansland, England](https://www.lingfieldunitedtrust.org/almshouses/)
