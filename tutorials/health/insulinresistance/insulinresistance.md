# Insulin Resistance


## Sub-title: "If you don't understand insulin, you don't understand fat loss"

Until 6 months ago I'd only thought of insulin as the hormone that regulates blood sugar. I'd thought, so long as insulin was effectively managing my blood glucose level and my blood tests (A1C) were "normal" all was well.

What I hadn't factored in is insulin resistance. It isn't tested in a standard suite of blood tests, so you won't know that you're affected by it.

Being insulin resistant means that your pancreas is working overtime, producing insulin constantly, and your body becomes somewhat immune to it.

Insulin resistance is triggered by eating carbs, sugar and processed foods, AND eating too frequently without letting your insulin levels drop (4+ hours). It triggers fatty liver, which alongside Insulin Resistance is also _not detected on a standard suite of blood tests (ALT and GGT)_.

Insulin resistance will also make you gain visceral fat, around your pancreas and organs.

Last night I listened to an old episode of British entrepreneur and investor Steven Bartlett's [The Diary Of A CEO podcast](https://stevenbartlett.com/doac). His guest was cardiologist Dr. Pradip Jamnadas.

[This 15 minute segment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FAtNs4hHXQQ) on insulin resistance really hit home. I'm pretty that I'd gotten myself into an insulin resistant state over the past 5 years which caused me to gain a lot of weight.


I was recently able to correct it, and become insulin-sensitve again, in under 2 months. Correcting it has allowed me to lose over 25 lbs of fat in 6 months.


If you don't have 15 minutes to watch that clip (or the entire episode) here are some of the pertinent quotes from Dr. Jamnadas:

> "... consuming glucose, consuming carbs every 2, 3 hours; I'm stimulating my pancreas, I'm stimulating my insulin. My insulin goes up, it comes down, BUT before it comes down it goes up again! ... So, the repeated consumption, and _frequent_ consumption of glucose is causing my insulin to stay up."


> "... the insulin stays higher for about 4 hours... _you become insulin resistant_."


> "Then you say, 'well it doesn't matter because the insulin is bringing the sugar level down, so what's the harm?' Your A1C is still good, you're not diabetic. Noooo, it's that background high insulin that is destroying your metabalism."


> "Wrong foods, and eating too frequently"


World renounded Canadian nephrologist, Dr. Jason Fung [has been preaching this for many years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0d5lJzMXnM), but in the past 2 to 3 years in particular, it's become a hot topic.
